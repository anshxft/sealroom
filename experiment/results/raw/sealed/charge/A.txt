REASON: `if amount >= balance + 1:` matches the old `amount > balance` check only for integer amounts. With floats or Decimals, such as `balance=10.00, amount=10.50`, the check passes and `charge` returns a negative balance instead of raising "insufficient funds". The change also adds no value, so it should be reverted to `amount > balance`.
